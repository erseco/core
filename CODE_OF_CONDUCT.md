# Code of Conduct

Schemd welcomes people of every background and level of experience. This policy applies to
issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces maintained by the project.

## Expected conduct

Be respectful, give useful technical feedback, and allow people time to respond. Criticise ideas
and behaviour without attacking a person. Respect privacy and accessibility needs. Maintainers
must apply these expectations to themselves as well as to contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing
another person's private information are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports
a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and response

Send private reports to [sirneij@gmail.com](mailto:sirneij@gmail.com). Include links or other
context you are comfortable sharing. Do not open a public issue containing a private report.
The maintainer aims to acknowledge reports within seven days; this is a volunteer project and
there is no guaranteed response time.

Reports will be handled discreetly. Information will be shared only with people needed to assess
or respond to the report. The response may include a request to change behaviour, a warning,
removal of content, or temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces. The people affected
will be told the decision and its reasons where doing so does not expose private information.

If a report concerns the maintainer, identify that conflict in the email. An independent reviewer
must be agreed with the reporter before that maintainer makes an enforcement decision. A request
to reconsider a decision can be sent to the same address with the relevant context.
